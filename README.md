# Gray Mines

An original, ad-free minesweeper-style puzzle that runs in the browser.

Open a cell to see how many mines touch it. Flag the cells you think hide mines, and clear the board without opening one. Easy (9×9, 10 mines), Medium (16×16, 40 mines), and Hard (30×16, 99 mines) are included. Tap or click to reveal; long-press, right-click, or Flag mode marks a mine (`F` toggles flag mode, `R` starts a new game). Best times stay on this device. After the first load, the app can be installed and played offline.

Play it at [https://bird-ops-ai.github.io/gray-mines/](https://bird-ops-ai.github.io/gray-mines/).
